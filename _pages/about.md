I am a PhD Candidate in Mathematics at **Indiana University Indianapolis (IU Indy)**, expecting to graduate as a **Purdue University** student in **May 2027**. Originally from Colombia, I completed my undergraduate studies at the **Universidad Industrial de Santander (UIS)**.

## Research Interests

My research sits at the intersection of complex dynamics and algebraic geometry. I primarily study the structure and dynamics of birational maps on $\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^2$. My recent projects focus on:
* Complex dynamics of birational plane maps arising from cluster algebra mutations.
* Holomorphic and random dynamics of free groups generated by Hénon-type maps.

I am highly motivated to expand my research scope toward higher-dimensional projective varieties, applying tools from rational dynamics to higher-dimensional complex algebraic structures.
