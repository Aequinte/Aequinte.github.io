In this paper, we study the birational maps on $\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^2$ obtained from mutations of cluster algebras. We analyze their dynamical invariants, including algebraic degrees and dynamical degrees, providing a geometric and complex dynamics perspective on these combinatorial objects.
