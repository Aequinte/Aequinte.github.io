The Ellis semigroup $E(X, f)$ is a compact topological semigroup associated with any discrete dynamical system $(X, f)$. It is a powerful tool used to study proximity and topological properties in dynamics. In this paper, we investigate the algebraic structure, cardinality, and conditions under which the Ellis semigroup forms a group, specifically focusing on cascades acting on compact metric countable spaces.
